## The verdict — five causes

1. **There is no design system, only utility classes.** 1,996 `rounded-*` in 30 distinct
   values; 7–10 radii per screen; 1,481 `font-black`, which is the default weight rather than
   emphasis; 15–28 text colours per dashboard screen; no webfont at all. Dark mode is ~180
   lines of `!important` overrides keyed to literal hex values, which is what a product looks
   like when its components carry no semantic colour.
2. **Every screen advertises itself before it works.** "Orders" four times before the first
   order; a 48px slogan 60% larger than the page's H1 and twice the revenue figure; three
   stacked headers before the first dish; two `<h1>`s on four guest screens.
3. **Colour and weight carry no meaning, so the loudest controls are the dangerous ones.**
   Tints bound to cards rather than values; Reject and Mark Paid are the highest-chroma
   controls on every order card while Accept renders black-on-black at ~1.1:1 in dark mode.
4. **The navigation and the vocabulary belong to the database.** 19 module-named sidebar
   items; four guest tabs reaching 4 of 16 screens; four vocabularies for one order
   lifecycle; seven strings for "not paid yet"; six names for the sticker on the wall; raw
   enums shown to guests.
5. **The empty and broken cases were never drawn.** 56 empty-state strings in two
   vocabularies, the blaming one used where nothing was filtered; no `not-found.tsx` or
   `error.tsx` anywhere; three of four staff accounts reporting a broken permission state in
   developer shorthand.

## What is kept

The order-tracking screen's waiting discipline and its state copy; the guest guide's type
ramp; the kitchen's three lanes and its existing TV Mode sizing; the two zero-states that
resolve themselves (`Add Default Services`, `Create the first full hotel backup`); the guest
requests screen's empty state; the POS total block; the two destructive actions that are
already gated behind a reason; the access-denied screen's recovery copy; the guide section's
"Continue exploring"; the optimistic updates, "Submitting…" swaps and toasts; all five
palettes, `next-themes` and `ThemePaletteProvider`; every route, data flow and real string.

## Not established

- `g04-payment` and `g10-service-thx` — unreachable without a live Xendit session and without
  any hotel service to request. Their shared bare-404 is ST-2; the screens themselves are not
  judged.
- Keyboard operation end to end, and a screen-reader pass, were not performed — the
  accessibility findings are measured from pixels and from source, not from driving the
  product with a keyboard or a reader. A11Y-10 and A11Y-11 predict what that pass would find.
